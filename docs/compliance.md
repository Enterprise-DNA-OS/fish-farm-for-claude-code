# The rules this system checks

`npm run farm -- compliance` (or `/compliance`) runs one check per rule below and cites the rule on every finding. The attention list (`/attention`) uses the same checks, ranked by how much they matter this week.

This is not legal advice. Each rule records what the law or your consent expects you to be able to show, with its source. Your own consent, licence, biosecurity plan and certification set the exact numbers: put them on the site (`feed_cap_t`, `max_density_kg_m3`, `min_do_mg_l`, `mass_mortality_pct`, `notify_within_days`, `notify_to`) and the checks follow them. If a rule here is out of date for your farm, tell Claude Code which rule and what changed; it updates this file and the check together.

| Rule | What it checks | Source | Where the number comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MORTALITY-REPORT` | A day's deaths in a unit above the site's threshold open a mortality event. The event must be reported to the people on `notify_to` within `notify_within_days`. Late or missing reports are findings. | Biosecurity Act 1993 (NZ), section 44: anyone who suspects an unwanted or notifiable organism must tell MPI as soon as possible (MPI exotic pest and disease hotline 0800 80 99 66). In Tasmania, the Animal Health Act 1995 makes listed diseases notifiable to the Chief Veterinary Officer. | The threshold and window in your biosecurity management plan or licence conditions. The demo uses 2% of a unit in a day, reported within one day. |
| `FEED-CAP` | Feed placed at a site over the last 365 days against the yearly feed limit in its consent or licence. | Marine farm resource consents under the Resource Management Act 1991 (NZ) commonly cap the tonnes of feed discharged a year. In Tasmania, environmental licences under the Environmental Management and Pollution Control Act 1994 set biomass or nitrogen output limits for each lease. | `feed_cap_t` on the site, from the consent condition. A rolling 365 days is stricter than any single consent year, so a clear result here is clear for the year your consent uses. |
| `DENSITY` | Stocking density (biomass over unit volume) above the unit's or site's limit. | Animal Welfare Act 1999 (NZ), section 10: the owner must meet the physical, health and behavioural needs of the animals. Density limits also sit in certification schemes and some consents. | `max_density_kg_m3` on the unit or site, from your welfare plan, certification or consent. |
| `LOW-OXYGEN` | Dissolved oxygen readings in the last seven days below the site minimum. | Animal Welfare Act 1999 (NZ), section 10, as above. | `min_do_mg_l` on the site, from your fish health plan. |
| `WITHHOLDING` | A harvest recorded inside the withholding period of a treatment given to those fish. The CLI refuses such a harvest; this check catches any that arrive by import or direct edit. | Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997 (NZ): treated animals must not be harvested for food until the withholding period on the product's registration or the veterinary authorisation has passed. The Animal Products Act 1999 requires the processor's programme to trace it. In Australia, withholding periods are set on APVMA product labels and permits. | `withholding_days` on each treatment, from the label or the vet's authorisation. |
| `VET-AUTHORISATION` | A treatment in the last year with no veterinary authorisation recorded. | Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997 (NZ): restricted veterinary medicines may only be used under a veterinarian's authorisation. | The vet and reference on each treatment. A product that needs no authorisation can carry the reference "not required". |
| `DAILY-RECORDS` | A stocked unit with no feed recorded for two days or more. | The daily production record your consent monitoring, your certification and your processor's traceability all rely on. | Fixed at two days; change it with `/customise`. |
| `BIOSECURITY-PLAN` | A site whose biosecurity management plan has not been reviewed in the last 365 days. | Resource Management (National Environmental Standards for Marine Aquaculture) Regulations 2020 (NZ): marine farm consents granted under these standards carry a biosecurity management plan. Review it as often as your consent says; this check uses once a year. | `biosecurity_plan_reviewed_on` on the site, set with `/review-plan`. |
